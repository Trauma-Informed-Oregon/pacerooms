    About P.A.C.E.

    P.A.C.E. Collective Steadiness is a brief, body-based practice for teams under pressure. In five to ten minutes, a team moves through four parts together: Presence, Attune, Connect, and Energy. No one is asked to share personal stories or explain how they feel.

    Two ways to use it

    Starter Pack. Free. The 12 core activities, with live breakout rooms for up to 15 groups.

    Organization license. The full library of 67 activities, up to 60 breakout rooms per session, sessions under your organization's name, and sign-in for your facilitators.

    Bring P.A.C.E. to your organization

    Licenses are arranged directly with Trauma Informed Oregon. Reach out to talk through your team's needs and pricing. Once your license is set up, your facilitators receive access by email.
